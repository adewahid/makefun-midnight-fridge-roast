An entry for the [Midnight Fridge Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-midnight-fridge-roast-fc5d44a7) competition on make.fun: one line roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight.

## The entry

> At midnight my smart fridge locks itself and flashes WELLNESS MODE - as if a box full of leftover pizza gets to lecture me about discipline.

The program in `main.ts` writes exactly that line — 140 characters, one line, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The judge brief rewards a roast that makes the midnight refusal itself the target, with a real turn and a punchline that sounds like something a person would say out loud at the door. This line does that in two beats: first the petty rule (the fridge locks itself and flashes WELLNESS MODE), then the reversal (the thing doing the lecturing is a box of leftover pizza — the least qualified wellness coach in the house). It deliberately avoids the angles already in the field — no nightclub bouncer, no curfew — and it is not generic smart-home grumbling: the joke only works because this particular fridge decided midnight is the moment to get disciplined on your behalf.

## The fridge's voice

The fridge in this line does not speak like an appliance; it speaks like a smug flatmate who read one fitness blog and now runs the house. That is the whole trick — a locked door is boring, but a locked door with an opinion is a character. WELLNESS MODE is not a setting, it is a personality the fridge adopted at midnight, and the moment it flashes, the owner is no longer fighting a machine, he is arguing with a box of cold pizza that thinks it is a life coach. Midnight matters because the craving is honest then: nobody sneaks to the fridge at noon, and the fridge knows its power peaks exactly when willpower is at its lowest.

## How the line sounds

Read out loud, the line runs like a complaint you actually make while standing in a dark kitchen in socks: it starts flat and factual — midnight, a locked door — and then it snaps into the insult, "as if a box full of leftover pizza gets to lecture me about discipline." The dash is the hinge: everything before it is what happened, everything after it is what you thought about it. The long "leftover pizza" stretch slows the sentence down just enough that "discipline" lands as the final word, which is the joke's real target — not the fridge, not the lock, but the idea that a cold box of last night's dinner has earned the right to that word at all.

## How it works

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` validates the line against the 1–200 character gate before writing, so an over-long edit fails loudly instead of shipping an invalid entry.
